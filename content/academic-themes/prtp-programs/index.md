## NAU offers this unique program for students pursuing parks & recreation degrees.

Welcome to Northern Arizona University’s Park Ranger Law Enforcement Academy (PRLEA), one of only a few nationally accredited programs that provide the training required to work as a seasonal park ranger in law enforcement in the National Park Service. Upon graduation from the program, students receive a “Certificate of Completion” issued by the National Park Service. This certificate is also recognized by the other land management agencies.

For more details see the [PRLEA’s external web page](https://lerangers.com/).
