## Biography
Marieke (Pronounced: Mar-ee-ka, it's a Dutch name!)

I was born and raised in Flagstaff, AZ and grew up in a very outdoors oriented family. We were always going on various adventures which instilled in me a desire to learn and a love for wild places at an early age.

I came to school at Northern Arizona University (NAU) in 1992 and graduated with a BS in Parks and Recreation Management (PRM) with an Outdoor Leadership emphasis in 1996.

After graduation I worked as a ski racing coach in New Mexico and started leading outdoor trips for numerous organizations. I started graduate school in Jackson Hole, WY in 1998 at the Teton Science School - Professional Residency in Environmental Education (PREE) Program.

After graduation in 1999 I took the National Outdoor Leadership School (NOLS) instructor course and worked as a field instructor leading primarily Outdoor Educator courses and backpacking trips.

During that time, I came back to NAU to continue my graduate work in Forestry and Environmental Education and started teaching in the PRM program through a graduate assistantship. Since I was back in Flagstaff, I decided to revisit my roots and started working in Grand Canyon as a river guide.

Both choices changed the course of life as I knew it. I fell in love with the Colorado River through Grand Canyon and teaching, so... the rest is history (kind of)!

I graduated from NAU with a MS in Forestry in 2002 and decided to live the seasonal lifestyle for a while; ski patrolling in the winter months and river guiding in the summer. I moved around to ski patrol at different mountains from Arizona to California to Colorado but always came back to Grand Canyon in the summer. I even started venturing up to the Chugach Mountains in Alaska to work as a Heli-ski guide, however my love for teaching started creeping back in.

During the summer of 2008 I found myself with an offer to come back to NAU as a full-time instructor in the Parks and Recreation Management program and decided it was time. It truly combines all the things I love in life; I can still ski (for fun), I can work in Grand Canyon in the summer, and I am teaching in a great program with wonderful & passionate students.

Other than teaching, skiing, and rafting my interests include mountain biking, canyoneering, painting/drawing, reading, woodworking, leather working, paragliding, whitewater kayaking and playing guitar.
