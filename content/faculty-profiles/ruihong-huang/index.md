## About

Dr. Ruihong (Ray) Huang is a Professor of geography in the Department of Geography, Planning, and Recreation at Northern Arizona University, where he has been a faculty member since 2003. His research focuses on GIS for urban transportation. He developed data models and schedule-base pathfinding algorithms for transit networks, and implemented the models and algorithms to online transit trip planners and urban accessibility studies.

## Research Interests

### Data models and pathfinding algorithms for transit network
- Development of object-oriented models for dynamic transit networks
- Database development for transit systems
- Development of schedule-based pathfinding algorithms using Pattern-First Search
- Development of web-based transit trip planners

### Transit-facilitated urban accessibility
- Understanding urban spatial structure and job accessibility
- Simulating individual work trips of transit-dependent commuters 
- Developing individual trip-based accessibility index
- Providing tools for individual work trip simulation

### Spatial data analysis
- Uncovering spatial patterns with Hidden Markov Model
- Analyzing urban land use and job accessibility
- Exploring national park accessibility and visitation
- Exploring space-time pattern of national park visitor activities
