## About
Dr. Folch's research centers on spatial data science, urban analytics, and the measurement of social and environmental inequality. His work examines how uncertainty, data quality, and spatial structure influence the interpretation of demographic, environmental, and administrative data.

## Research Interests
### Spatial data quality and uncertainty
- Spatial uncertainty essessment
- Data quality assessment
- Contextual measurement
- Regionalization methods
- Geographic and temporal linkage

### Urban Analytics, Segregation, and Social Structure
- Understanding urban form, social vulnerability, segregation, and occupational patterns
- Examines how spatial structure shapes inequality and urban dynamics

### Applied Spatial Analysis in Public Health and Environment
- Applied research linking spatial data to environmental exposures, health outcomes, and risk assessment.
- Investigating how spatial proximity, environmental hazards, and data uncertainty influence public health findings.
