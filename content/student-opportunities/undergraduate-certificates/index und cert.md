## Undergraduate Certificate Programs

The School of Earth and Sustainability offers specialized undergraduate certificate programs designed to provide focused training and expertise in specific areas of environmental science, geology, and sustainability. These programs are perfect for students looking to:

- **Complement their major** with specialized skills
- **Enhance career prospects** with targeted expertise  
- **Explore new fields** without committing to a full degree program
- **Build credentials** for graduate school or career advancement

## Available Programs

### Certificate programs are currently being developed

We are in the process of developing undergraduate certificate programs in high-demand areas such as:

#### Environmental Monitoring & Assessment
- Water quality monitoring techniques
- Air quality assessment methods
- Soil contamination evaluation
- Environmental data analysis

#### Sustainability & Climate Action
- Climate change adaptation strategies
- Renewable energy systems
- Sustainable business practices
- Carbon footprint assessment

#### Geospatial Analysis & Remote Sensing
- GIS applications in environmental science
- Remote sensing data interpretation
- Spatial data analysis techniques
- Mapping and visualization skills

#### Field Methods in Earth Sciences
- Geological field techniques
- Environmental sampling methods
- Field instrumentation and data collection
- Safety protocols for fieldwork

## Program Features

### Flexible Scheduling
- Most courses offered evenings and weekends
- Hybrid online/in-person options available
- Self-paced learning components
- Summer intensive options

### Hands-On Learning
- Laboratory experiences
- Field work opportunities  
- Real-world project applications
- Professional internship connections

### Industry Connections
- Guest lectures from professionals
- Networking opportunities
- Job placement assistance
- Professional certification preparation

## Admission Requirements

### General Requirements
- **High School Diploma:** or equivalent
- **GPA Requirement:** Minimum 2.5 cumulative GPA
- **Prerequisites:** Varies by program (typically basic math and science courses)
- **Application:** Simple online application process

### For Current NAU Students
- Certificate programs can be completed alongside degree programs
- Credits may count toward degree requirements (with advisor approval)
- Priority registration for certificate courses

## Career Benefits

Undergraduate certificates provide:
- **Enhanced employability** in competitive job markets
- **Specialized skills** valued by employers
- **Professional networking** opportunities
- **Foundation** for advanced study or graduate certificates
- **Career pivot** opportunities into environmental fields

## Application Process

### How to Apply
1. Complete online application
2. Submit unofficial transcripts
3. Meet with academic advisor
4. Register for certificate courses

### Application Timeline
- **Rolling admissions** - apply anytime
- **Early registration** for fall and spring semesters
- **Summer programs** - apply by May 1st
