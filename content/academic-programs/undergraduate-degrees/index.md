## Undergraduate Degree Programs

The Department of Geography, Planning and Recreation offers undergraduate degree programs designed to provide students with comprehensive knowledge and hands-on experience in geographic science, environment and society, urban planning, parks and recreation management, and community sustainability studies.

### Program Overview
- **Total Units:** 120
- **Major Requirements:** 36-39 units
- **Minor Requirements:** 18-24 units
- **Major GPA:** C

### Description
The Bachelor of Science in Geography, Environment and Society integrates geographic knowledge and GIS mapping technologies with the problem-solving fields of community planning and urban design. This educational framework prepares students for careers in creating more livable and sustainable communities, while contributing to a better world.

### Key Features
- Inegrated geographic and spatial analysis foundation
- Applied emphasis alighed with career pathways
- Focus on creating just, sustainable, and livable communities
- Professional inquiry, communication, and research skills

- **Total Units:** 120
- **Major GPA:** C
- **Highest Mathematics Requirement:** STA 270
- **Fieldwork Experience/Internship:** Required

### Description
The Parks and Recreation Management (PRM) program fulfills the discipline of preparing students for careers in parks, recreation, tourism, and related fields. The program provides an equitable, inclusive, and justice-oriented approach to education that develops students' critical thinking skills and competencies needed for these careers.

### Emphases
- Community, Commericial & Tourism Emphasis
- Outdoor Education & Leadership Emphasis

### Career Focus

- Adventure leadership and guiding
- Community recreation and event management

### Program Overview
- **GPA:** 3.25(+) at the time of application; 3.5(+) for the recent 12 units of GSP or PRM courses
- **When to Apply:** junior or first half of senior year
- **Letters of support:** 2

### Description
Northern Arizona University offers this unique option to highly qualified and mature students who are looking to get a head start on their Master of Science in Applied Geospatial Sciences degree (Master of Science in Geography starting Fall 2022). In this program, you are allowed to take up to twelve units at the 400-level or higher to apply toward both degrees, allowing you to earn both your BS and MS in only five years.
