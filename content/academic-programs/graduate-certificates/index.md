## Geographic Information Systems (GIS) Certificate

### Program Overview
- **Delivery:** Asynchronous Online
- **Credits:** 16
- **Duration:** Two semesters

### Program Description
The Geographic Information Systems (GIS) Certificate prepares students to use geospatial technologies in professional settings. GIS provides a framework for collecting, managing, and analyzing spatial data, integrating multiple data types to create maps, visualizations, and 3D scenes. Through advanced coursework in GIS principles, enterprise geodatabases, geospatial programming, and web‑based GIS, students gain practical skills applicable to urban planning, cartography, remote sensing, and environmental resource management. Designed for post‑graduate learners entering the GIS field or adding geospatial analysis to their discipline, the certificate equips graduates for careers in landscape architecture, local government, scientific research, community health, surveying, and related fields.

### Curriculum
- GSP 520 - Principles of Remote Sensing
- GSP 531 - Map Design and GIS
- GSP 535 - Programming for GIS
- GSP 536 - Enterprise Geodatabase Development and Applications
- GSP 538 - Geographic Data Analysis
- GSP 539 - Geovisualization
- GSP 540 - GIS for Transprotation

### Student Learning Outcomes:
- Create, manipulate and manage geospatial data.
- Perform spatial analysis to support spatial related decision making.
- Develop GIS tools and automate geoprocessing.
- Present and deliver geospatial information in online and professional quality formats.

### Admission Requirements:
- Transcripts
- Undergraduate degree from a regionally accredited institution with 3.0 GPA on a 4.0 scale or the equivalent.
