Peter Friederici

#3 Biography Peter Friederici is a journalist and writer who teaches in the NAU School of Communication, with a focus on science communication, and also teaches in the university’s Sustainable Communities program. His books, essays, and articles have focused on numerous aspects of connections between humans and nature. His most recent book is Beyond Climate Breakdown: Envisioning New Stories of Radical Hope, published in 2022 by MIT Press. He also directs the Sustainable Communities Program’s Rural Foods Pathways Project, which seeks to improve food production, access, and sustainability across Northern Arizona.
